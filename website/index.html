What it does

Local-first

All data lives in a SQLite database on your machine. Nothing is sent to any server — ever. Your usage history is completely private.

Desktop agent

A background tray process polls the active window at 1 Hz, detecting idle time and accumulating per-app minute buckets automatically.

Browser extension

The Chrome extension tracks active tab domains — not full URLs — and syncs data to the local agent over loopback via a pairing token.

Soft limits

Set daily time limits on any app or domain. Native desktop notifications fire at 50%, 80% and 100% of your limit — once per threshold per day.

Mobile companion

Track your phone
too.

FocusLens syncs with your phone over your local Wi-Fi network. No internet connection required — your data never touches a cloud server.

🤖
Android
Native app tracks all installed apps in the background using the Android UsageStatsManager API. Syncs with your desktop over Wi-Fi.

iPhone / iPad
Apple restricts third-party access to Screen Time data. iOS companion can view your desktop stats but cannot track phone app usage. Install as a home screen web app via Safari.
